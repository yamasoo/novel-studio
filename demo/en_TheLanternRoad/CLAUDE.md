# "The Lantern Road" writing project

A mapmaker's apprentice and a lantern that only lights roads the maps swear don't exist set out to find her vanished master.

> This is the sample book that ships with Novel Studio. Its content only shows what the pages look like. You can delete it at any time.

## Basics
- Genre: road fantasy adventure, light mystery　Platform: personal blog serial　Expected length: novella, about 120,000 words
- Viewpoint: third person limited, past tense, following Wren
- Chapter length: 2,500-3,500 words (the sample chapters are deliberately short)
- Language: English. American spelling, curly quotes for dialogue, em dashes without spaces, ellipses as a single character. Input in another language is translated into English before it is written into files.

## Where things are
- Settings (static): `設定/`　story core, style, author preferences, glossary, worldbuilding, characters, factions; unconfirmed items in `設定/草稿/`
- Plans (not yet happened): `大綱/`　whole-book outline, volume outlines, chapter cards
- Facts (already happened): `追蹤/`　state card, character state, foreshadowing, timeline, chapter summaries
- Chapter text: `正文/第NNN章_<title>.md`; candidate drafts: `候選/`
- Blank templates: `_範本/`

(Folder and file names stay in Chinese; the studio program reads them.)

## Iron rules
1. Keep plans and facts apart: the outline is plan, tracking is fact. Do not change tracking before the chapter text is final.
2. Keep drafts and official apart: chapter text may cite only official settings, never `設定/草稿/`.
3. The chapter card is the only plot blueprint for the chapter. The text does not change results the card has settled.
4. When writing, obey "what the reader knows now" in `追蹤/時間線.md`; do not spoil early.

## Three tiers of new things
- **Just write it**: passers-by, one-off props, scene detail, fine detail of place names.
- **Write it and report it**: a named supporting character, a new faction, a new rule or demonstration of the lantern's power, new foreshadowing, a new relationship or promise, a place or object that will recur.
- **Ask the author first**: a new main-line event or big twist, the rules of the lantern, revealing what happened to Orrin or paying off important foreshadowing, changing a result the chapter card settled, anything that conflicts with existing settings.
- When unsure: treat it as "write it and report it" while writing, and "ask the author first" while planning.

## Style priority
**the author's request this time > `設定/文風.md` > `設定/作者偏好.md` > general writing rules**

## How we work
- Skills used: starting a book and style → novel-setup; settings database → novel-bible; outline and cards → novel-outline; chapters and revision → novel-write; consistency review → novel-review; removing AI voice → novel-deslop.
- Writing chapter text needs the author to say which chapter, or "continue". At most 3 chapters at a time.
- Reports to the author in plain words: what was written, what needs a decision, what is next.
- When the author says "from now on …", record it in `設定/作者偏好.md`.

## Special rules for this book
- Wren has no combat skill. Every win comes from counting, memory and noticing.
- In volume one the lantern only shows roads. It has no other power.
