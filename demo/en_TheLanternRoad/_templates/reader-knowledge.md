# Reader knowledge

> What the reader believes so far: "known" is what the reader knows; "question" is a question the reader is waiting to have answered; "misbelief" is what the reader wrongly believes (for reversals).
> "Author's truth" is the answer (optional); a blank "Resolved in" means not yet revealed. When writing, do not resolve a question or misbelief before its time.
> Use the type words known / question / misbelief exactly as given; the studio reads them (it also understands the Chinese ones).
