# Novel project structure and database specification

This specification is the common foundation of the whole novel-* skill set. novel-setup builds folders by it, and every other skill reads and writes files by its paths.

**Important:** folder names, file names, YAML keys, table headers and the fixed words in tables (statuses, types) stay in Chinese exactly as written here, because the novel studio program reads them. The content you write into them (names, descriptions, summaries, notes) is in English.

## 1. Folder structure

```
{Title}/
├── CLAUDE.md                  ← rules for this book (loaded at the start of every conversation)
├── 設定/                      ← static settings: change rarely, consulted before writing
│   ├── 故事核心.md            genre, selling points, one-line hook, story engine, reader contract, ending direction
│   ├── 文風.md                this book's style: viewpoint, sentence rhythm, taboos, example passages
│   ├── 作者偏好.md            habits the author has said "from now on" about
│   ├── 名詞表.md              proper nouns, forms of address, fixed spellings
│   ├── 世界觀/{topic}.md      one topic per file: geography, history, power system, social structure …
│   ├── 角色/{name}.md         one person per file: static profile
│   ├── 勢力/{name}.md         one organization per file
│   └── 草稿/                  unconfirmed settings (AI-generated, extracted from conversation); moved to the official folders once confirmed
├── 大綱/                      ← plans: what has not yet happened
│   ├── 全書大綱.md
│   ├── 卷綱_第一卷.md
│   └── 章綱/第001章.md        one file per chapter
├── 正文/
│   └── 第001章_<title>.md
├── 追蹤/                      ← facts that have happened: updated only after a chapter is written
│   ├── 狀態卡.md              the current situation, required reading before every chapter
│   ├── 角色狀態/{name}.md     dynamic snapshot: location, injuries, holdings, what they know
│   ├── 伏筆.md                foreshadowing table
│   ├── 時間線.md              the author's truth and what the reader knows
│   ├── 讀者認知.md            what the reader knows, suspects and misbelieves (optional; created during write-back)
│   ├── 關係.md                who stands where toward whom, with history (optional; created during write-back)
│   └── 章節摘要/第001章.md    one short summary per chapter
├── 候選/                      candidates: chapters written but not yet adopted by the author (when using the review screen)
├── 範例/                      passages the author likes, used as positive style examples (optional)
└── _範本/                     blank templates for each kind of file (copied in by novel-setup)
```

Chapter numbers are zero-padded to three digits (第001章), four digits past chapter 999. Characters, factions and places are filed under their English names (設定/角色/Ines Marlow.md).

## 2. Three iron rules

1. **Keep plans and facts apart.** The outline and cards say "what is meant to happen"; tracking says "what has already happened in the text". Tracking is not changed before the text is written, and an outline plan is never cited as established fact.
2. **Keep settings and state apart.** `設定/角色/` holds the profile that rarely changes (personality, looks, background, way of speaking); `追蹤/角色狀態/` holds what may change every chapter (where they are, what injuries, what they carry, which secrets they know, how they stand with whom).
3. **Keep drafts and official apart.** Settings generated by AI or organised from conversation that the author has not approved always go to `設定/草稿/` first. They move to the official folders only after the author confirms. Chapter text may cite only official settings.

## 3. YAML frontmatter specification

Every database file starts with a YAML block, for searching and checking. Write `null` or an empty list for a missing value; do not delete the key.

### Character (設定/角色/{name}.md)

```yaml
---
類型: 角色
名稱: Ines Marlow
別名: [Nessie, Miss Ines]
定位: 主角          # 主角 / 主要配角 / 配角 / 反派 / 路人
首次登場: 第001章
狀態: 正式          # 正式 / 草稿
---
```

### Faction, worldbuilding

```yaml
---
類型: 勢力          # or 世界觀
名稱: The Quay Guild
狀態: 正式
相關角色: [Ines Marlow, Callum Brandt]
---
```

### Chapter card (大綱/章綱/第001章.md)

```yaml
---
類型: 章綱
章號: 1
章名: The Day the Engagement Ended
所屬卷: 第一卷
字數目標: 2500      # may be empty
狀態: 已確認        # 草稿 / 已確認 / 已寫
---
```

### Character state (追蹤/角色狀態/{name}.md)

```yaml
---
類型: 角色狀態
名稱: Ines Marlow
更新至: 第012章
最後登場: 第012章
---
```

### Chapter summary (追蹤/章節摘要/第001章.md)

```yaml
---
類型: 章節摘要
章號: 1
章名: The Day the Engagement Ended
字數: 2600
---
```

(For an English book the `字數` field counts words.)

## 4. How to write the tracking files

### 狀態卡.md (fixed seven sections; keep the whole file under 1,500 words)

1. **目前位置**: which volume, which chapter just finished, in-story time, main setting
2. **主線進度**: how far the main line has got and what the next goal is
3. **在場角色**: the characters now at the centre of the story, one sentence of news each
4. **下一章承諾**: what the last chapter's ending leaves for the next chapter to catch
5. **長期約束**: rules to keep through the whole book (e.g. the lead cannot reveal her identity before volume three)
6. **連續性風險**: error-prone details (e.g. Callum's left hand is still injured)
7. **活躍伏筆**: IDs of foreshadowing to advance or pay off now, with one line each

The state card is a snapshot of "now", not a history. Delete outdated content directly; history stays in the chapter summaries.

### 伏筆.md (one table)

| 編號 | 內容 | 埋設章 | 預計回收 | 最後出現 | 狀態 | 重要度 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F001 | The half ferry token her mother left | 第001章 | end of volume one | 第008章 | 推進中 | 高 |

Status must be one of: 已埋, 推進中, 已回收, 廢棄. Importance: 高, 中, 低. "Planned payoff" holds only the one chapter you mean to pay it off in (第NNN章); leave it empty if undecided. Arrangements like "advance from chapter N" or "echo in chapter N" go in the content column or the outline, otherwise the studio would treat them as the payoff chapter when judging overdue items (the studio skips such wording, but a bare chapter number is the most reliable).

### 時間線.md (the author's truth and what the reader knows, in separate columns)

| 編號 | 故事時間 | 實際發生（作者真相） | 讀者目前知道 | 揭示章 | 相關角色 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E001 | Three winters ago | Ines's father was framed by the Brandts | Only knows her father went to prison | 未揭示 | her father, old Mr. Brandt |

"What the reader knows now" is the information boundary while writing: the text must not let a character or the narrator say what the reader should not yet know.

### 讀者認知.md (the reader's state of mind, optional)

| 編號 | 類型 | 內容 | 作者真相 | 種下章 | 回收章 | 相關角色 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R001 | 懸念 | Why did her father plead guilty? | The Brandts threatened her mother's life | 第003章 |  | her father, old Mr. Brandt |
| R002 | 誤認 | The reader thinks young Brandt sent the letter | It was the Brandts' old servant | 第005章 | 第012章 | Callum |

Type must be one of: 已知 (the reader knows, but some characters in the story do not yet), 懸念 (a question the reader waits to have answered), 誤認 (what the reader wrongly believes). A blank resolving chapter means not yet revealed; the author's truth is optional. The timeline records events; this table records the reader's questions and misunderstandings.

### 關係.md (between characters, who toward whom, optional)

| 編號 | 誰 | 對誰 | 關係 | 態度 | 起始章 | 結束章 | 變化原因 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L001 | Callum | Ines | classmates | dismissive | 第003章 | 第020章 | first meeting |
| L002 | Callum | Ines | allies | respectful | 第020章 |  | Ines took the blame for him |
| L003 | Ines | Callum | classmates | wary | 第003章 |  | first meeting |

One direction per row: A toward B and B toward A are separate, and the two views may differ. When a relationship changes, put the changing chapter in the old row's end chapter and open a new row; never overwrite, so the history stays. A blank end chapter means currently valid. When writing, look only at the currently valid rows among characters on stage; when reviewing, read the history too, to catch "changed without a cause" or "slipped back".

### 章節摘要/第NNN章.md (100-250 words)

Four things: what happened in the chapter, how the situation changed, what and who newly appeared, where the chapter stops.

## 5. Who owns each file

| File | Main writer | When |
|---|---|---|
| 設定/故事核心, 文風 | novel-setup, novel-outline | at setup, when changing direction |
| 設定/作者偏好 | any skill | when the author clearly says "from now on …" |
| 設定/角色, 勢力, 世界觀, 名詞表, 草稿 | novel-bible | when building settings, and registering new things after a chapter |
| 大綱/* | novel-outline | when planning |
| 正文/* | novel-write | when writing chapters |
| 追蹤/* | novel-write (write-back after writing), novel-review (fixing errors) | after the chapter text is done |

Tracking files are updated only once the chapter text is final. When the author revises a chapter already written, use novel-write's revision flow and fix tracking along with it.
