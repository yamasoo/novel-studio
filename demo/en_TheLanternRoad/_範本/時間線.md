# 時間線

> "Actually happened" is the truth the author knows; "what the reader knows now" is the information boundary while writing.
> The text must not let a character or the narrator reveal what the reader should not yet know.
> Record only events that have happened or been mentioned in the chapter text; future plans go in the outline.

| 編號 | 故事時間 | 實際發生（作者真相） | 讀者目前知道 | 揭示章 | 相關角色 |
