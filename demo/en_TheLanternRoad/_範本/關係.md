# 角色關係

> Who stands where "toward" whom, one direction per row (A toward B and B toward A are separate rows). "Relationship" is role or standing (master and pupil, allies, creditor); "attitude" is this person's feeling and warmth toward the other (trust, wariness, affection).
> When a relationship changes, fill the end chapter on the old row and open a new row; do not overwrite. A blank end chapter means currently valid. A blank start chapter means it has been so from the beginning or is uncertain.
