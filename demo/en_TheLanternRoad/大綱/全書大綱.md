# 全書大綱

## 總覽
- Expected volumes and length: two parts, about 120,000 words
- Main line in one sentence: Wren goes from a shop clerk who copies maps to the one who redraws them, by walking the road the Guild erased
- Ending: the road is restored to the master map in public, and Orrin stands beside her

## 分卷

### 第一卷：The Erased Road

- Volume goal: reach Greywater Bridge and learn why the road was erased
- Main opponent: Warden-Surveyor Corvin Tarr and his clerk Dessa Pike
- Change by the end of the volume: Wren holds proof the road existed
- Big climax at the end: the night at Greywater Bridge

### 第二卷：The Master Map
- Length: chapters 31-60
- Volume goal: redraw the master map in public
