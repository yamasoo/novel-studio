# 卷綱：The Erased Road

## 卷契約
- Selling point of this volume: a road that appears only to someone who has earned it, and a lantern with opinions
- The lead's goal this volume: reach Greywater Bridge and find out what the Guild erased
- Opponent this volume: Corvin Tarr and Dessa Pike
- Payoff at the end: Wren lights the whole bridge road in front of a Guild inspector
- New hook at the end: Hob admits who carried him down the road before

## 本卷常駐規則
- The lantern only shows roads in this volume

## 劇情單元

### 單元一：The Shop and the Stove（第001～005章）
- Starting situation: the shop is quiet, Orrin has been gone three weeks
- Conflict: the Guild wants Orrin's survey books, and Wren wants to keep them
- Small climax: Wren lights Hob for the first time and the road appears
- Result and cost: she leaves town with the lantern, and the Guild notices the empty shop
