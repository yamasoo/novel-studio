# 第1章 The Missing Milestone

## 本章四問
1. What the lead wants to do in this chapter: keep Orrin's survey books out of the Guild's hands
2. Who or what stops them: Dessa Pike arrives with a Guild warrant
3. What the situation is after this chapter: the books are gone, but Wren has found a lantern that speaks
4. Why the reader will keep reading: which road did Orrin tell her to forget?

## 本章功能與目標情緒
- Function: set up
- Main feeling for the reader: unease turning to curiosity

## 情節點
1. Wren inks a town map in the quiet shop, three weeks after Orrin vanished
2. Dessa Pike arrives to take Orrin's survey books and says the Guild has no record of any road past Hollin Cross
3. Wren notices the old map's margin counts milestones 39, 40, 42, with 41 missing
4. After Dessa leaves, Wren finds a brass lantern behind the stove with a note in Orrin's hand
5. The lantern speaks

## 章末鉤子
- Type: open question: "Which road did he tell you to forget?"
