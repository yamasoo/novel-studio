# 第2章 Names You Have Walked

## 本章四問
1. What the lead wants to do in this chapter: make the lantern work
2. Who or what stops them: Hob will not light for a place she has only read about
3. What the situation is after this chapter: Wren lights Hob with "Pellman's Ford" and sees a silver road she once erased
4. Why the reader will keep reading: why did Orrin make her erase it?

## 本章功能與目標情緒
- Function: reveal information
- Main feeling for the reader: wonder with a sting

## 情節點
1. Wren tries to light Hob with "Hollin Cross" and "Greywater"; the glass stays dark
2. Hob explains the name rule
3. She remembers wading Pellman's Ford at twelve and speaks the name; the light comes on
4. A silver line leaves the north gate where the map shows only fields
5. Wren remembers erasing exactly that line from the master map last spring

## 章末鉤子
- Type: reversal: the road she erased is the one lighting up
