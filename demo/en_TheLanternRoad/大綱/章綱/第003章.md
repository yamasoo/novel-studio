# 第3章 The First Milestone

## 本章四問
1. What the lead wants to do in this chapter: follow the silver line to its first landmark
2. Who or what stops them: her own nerves, and Hob's warnings
3. What the situation is after this chapter: she finds a carved milestone and a fresh survey stake with Orrin's mark
4. Why the reader will keep reading: Orrin walked this road after he vanished

## 本章功能與目標情緒
- Function: advance the main line
- Main feeling for the reader: tension, then hope

## 情節點
1. Wren leaves at dawn and follows the silver line across wet fields
2. She finds a half-sunk milestone carved with a lantern
3. Beside it stands a survey stake, painted this week
4. Hob says someone near him is lying; the glass fogs when Wren says she is fine
5. The stake carries Orrin's initials, not the Guild's

## 章末鉤子
- Type: reversal: Orrin marked the road himself
