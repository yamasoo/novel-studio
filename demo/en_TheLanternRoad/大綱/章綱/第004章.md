# 第4章 The Toll House

## 本章四問
1. What the lead wants to do in this chapter: learn whether Orrin passed the toll house
2. Who or what stops them: the keeper will not talk to anyone from the Guild's side of the river
3. What the situation is after this chapter: the keeper remembers Orrin and gives Wren a name to light
4. Why the reader will keep reading: the name is a place Wren has never been

## 本章功能與目標情緒
- Function: reveal information
- Main feeling for the reader: curiosity

## 情節點
1. Wren reaches a shuttered toll house at midday
2. The keeper says the road has no toll because it has no traffic
3. Wren shows Orrin's note and the keeper softens
4. The keeper says Orrin left a message
5. The message names a place Wren has not stood in

## 章末鉤子
- Type: open question: why would Orrin leave her a place she cannot light?
