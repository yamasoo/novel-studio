# The Lantern

## 一句話
Hob's light shows roads that have been erased from every map, but only to someone who has earned the way.

## 規則
- Hob lights only when the person holding him speaks the name of a place they have truly stood in
- Once lit, Hob burns until the next sunrise, or until a lie puts him out
- Within about twenty paces, erased roads show as faint silver lines on the ground
- Only people standing inside the light can see the lines
- The line fades as soon as the light moves on

## 代價與限制
- Each lighting leaves the holder cold and hungry for an hour
- A lie spoken near Hob fogs the glass and dims the light
- In volume one the lantern shows roads and nothing else

## 讀者何時知道
- The name rule and the silver lines: chapter 2
- Who carried Hob down the road before: end of volume one
