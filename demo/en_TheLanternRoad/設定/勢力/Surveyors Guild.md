# Surveyors' Guild

## 一句話
The official map authority of the Marches, which has quietly struck one road from every map.

## 基本資料
- Size and territory: survey halls in every market town of the Marches
- Leader: Warden-Surveyor Corvin Tarr
- Main members: clerks, rod-bearers, a few field surveyors
- Resources and methods: the only maps courts and merchants will accept

## 目標與利益
- What it wants: for no one to ask about Greywater Bridge
- Who it is in conflict with: independent mapmakers, especially Orrin Vale

## 對主角的態度
Sees Wren as a harmless clerk and has not yet noticed the lantern.
