# 故事核心

## 一句話鉤子
A mapmaker's apprentice and a lantern that only lights roads the maps swear don't exist set out to find her vanished master.

## 題材與定位
- Genre: fantasy adventure with a light mystery thread
- Target readers: readers of cozy-but-curious road fantasy
- Platform and update rhythm: personal blog, two chapters a week
- Expected length: about 120,000 words in two parts
- Viewpoint: third person limited, past tense, following Wren

## 賣點（讀者為什麼看）
1. A road that appears only to the one person who has earned it
2. A dry-voiced lantern who knows more than he says
3. Slow reveal of what the Surveyors' Guild erased from the map, and why

## 主角
- What they want (outer goal): find Master Orrin Vale, who vanished three weeks ago
- What they really need (inner growth): to trust her own eyes instead of copying what she is told
- Biggest flaw or limit: a copyist's habit of redrawing whatever she is handed, and no field experience
- Special advantage (if any): a perfect memory for distances, and Hob the lantern

## 故事引擎
Every place name Wren has truly walked lets Hob light another stretch of an erased road. Each stretch answers one question about Orrin and opens a new one, and each time the Guild moves a step closer to stopping her.

## 核心衝突
- Main opponent: Warden-Surveyor Corvin Tarr of the Surveyors' Guild
- Root of the conflict: three years ago the Guild struck the road from every official map to hide what happened at Greywater Bridge

## 讀者契約
- Wren never turns into a swordswoman
- The lantern never solves a problem for her; it only shows the way
- The mystery is fair: every clue the answer needs is on the page

## 終局方向
Wren redraws the master map in public with the road restored; Orrin chooses to stand beside her instead of staying hidden.

## 升級台階
1. Hollin Cross (the shop, the Guild clerk)
2. The Lantern Road's first stretch (milestones, toll house)
3. Greywater Bridge and the Guild's survey hall
