# 本書文風

> This file outranks every general writing rule.
> Core in one sentence: a warm, dry, close-in voice that notices small physical details and lets the lantern's understatement carry the humor.

## 定位與參考
- Genre and readers: road fantasy with a mystery thread, read mostly on phones
- Not like: not a grimdark epic; not a parody

## 基本設定
- Viewpoint: third limited, following Wren, one viewpoint per scene
- Tense and distance: past tense, close to Wren; thoughts shown in plain narration, no italics
- Language and region: English (American spelling)
- Punctuation: curly double quotes for dialogue, curly single quotes inside them; em dash without spaces; ellipsis as one character

## 句子與節奏
- Mostly short to medium sentences; tension gets shorter, never longer
- At most three sentences of description before returning to action or dialogue

## 對白
- Wren speaks plainly, often in questions, and counts things aloud when nervous
- Hob is dry and brief; he understates
- Tags are "said" or "asked", or an action beat

## 情緒寫法
- Never name the emotion; use a counted pace, a stained thumb, a held breath
- One plain line of feeling per chapter at most

## 本書禁忌
- No omniscient teaser lines
- No reflection at chapter ends; end on a concrete open question

## 允許的例外
- Hob's catchphrase "Noted." may repeat; it marks him
- Wren counting paces aloud may repeat
