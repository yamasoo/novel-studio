# Hob

## 一句話
A battered brass lantern with a dry voice and a long memory for roads.

## 外在
- Age / status: older than the Guild, by his own account
- Distinguishing looks: dented brass, a cracked left pane, a handle worn smooth

## 內在
- Wants: to see the Lantern Road walked again
- Fears: the light going cold for good
- Flaw: tells the truth in small pieces, and only when asked
- Bottom line: will not light a road for someone who lies to him
- Secret: he remembers who first carried him down the road, and has not said

## 說話方式（聲音卡）
- Habitual sentence patterns: understatement, short declaratives
- Catchphrase: "Noted."
- Reaction when pushed: goes quiet; the glass fogs
- Speaks to everyone as if they were slightly late

## 關係
- Wren Alder: reluctant trust that grows
- Master Orrin Vale: carried him once; Hob has not said how that ended

## 角色弧線
From a lantern that answers questions to one that volunteers the truth.
