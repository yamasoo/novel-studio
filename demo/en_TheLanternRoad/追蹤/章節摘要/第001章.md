# 第1章 The Missing Milestone

## 發生了什麼
Dessa Pike of the Surveyors' Guild takes Orrin Vale's survey books and says the Guild has no record of any road past Hollin Cross. After she leaves, Wren notices the old map is missing milestone 41, then finds a speaking lantern behind the stove.

## 局面變化
The Guild now holds Orrin's records; Wren holds the lantern and his note.

## 新出現的人事物
Dessa Pike, Hob, Orrin's note.

## 結尾停在
Hob asks which road Orrin told her to forget.
