# 第2章 Names You Have Walked

## 發生了什麼
Hob explains that he lights only for a place Wren has truly stood in. She lights him with "Pellman's Ford" and sees a silver road leaving the north gate. She remembers erasing exactly that line from the master map last spring.

## 局面變化
Wren knows the road is real and that she helped hide it.

## 新出現的人事物
Pellman's Ford, the name rule, the silver road.

## 結尾停在
The road she erased is waiting outside the window.
