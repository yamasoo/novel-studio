# Third-party notices (plugin-en)

The English skills in this folder are original work written for this project.
Their structure (separate skills for setup, outline, drafting, review and revision) was informed by
publicly available MIT-licensed fiction-writing skill collections, notably `story-skills`;
no text was copied from them. The lists of AI-writing tells and all examples are our own wording,
based on patterns widely described by writers and editors.
