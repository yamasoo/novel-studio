# Consistency checklist

## A. Six in-chapter items (check every chapter)

### 1. Plot logic
- Is there enough set-up for the most critical event?
- Is there enough trigger for the most critical decision?
- After the event, did the situation really change, and did the consequences land?
- Was a problem solved by coincidence? (Coincidence may create trouble but should not resolve it.)

### 2. Character goals
- Is it clear what the lead wants in this chapter?
- Does the goal drift for no reason? When it changes, is there a trigger and a reason?

### 3. Emotion and relationships
- Do the emotional changes have a trigger and a transition? Any flip into hostility or friendship in one beat?
- Does the warmth of the relationship connect with the last scene and the last chapter?

### 4. Physical and information state
- Did an injury, fatigue, poison, pregnancy or drug effect vanish for no reason?
- Is what a character knows consistent with what they "should know"? Does anyone know something they could not? Does anyone forget what they clearly knew?
- Is it consistent who holds each object?

### 5. Scene and transitions
- Will the reader get lost? Are time, place and viewpoint clear?
- Did the last scene's emotional momentum carry over?

### 6. Chapter-ending hand-off
- Does the ending rest on a change?
- Did this chapter catch the previous chapter's ending hook?
- Can the next chapter's first beat catch this ending? (Compare with the next card, if there is one.)

## B. Cross-chapter and database (batch reviews, or when asked)

### 7. Setting consistency
- Do abilities, rules, ranks, prices and geographic distances in the text agree with `設定/世界觀/`?
- Do characters' looks, status, background and way of speaking agree with `設定/角色/`?
- Do terms, forms of address and spellings agree with `設定/名詞表.md`? (One term spelled two ways, one person addressed wrongly.)
- Does the text use unconfirmed content from `設定/草稿/`?

### 8. Does tracking keep up with the text?
- Do the `更新至` of the chapter summaries, state card, character state, foreshadowing and timeline reach the latest chapter?
- Spot check: are the important facts that happened in the text in tracking? Did what tracking records really happen in the text?

### 9. Foreshadowing health
- Foreshadowing of high or medium importance whose "last appearance" is more than 10 chapters ago: list it and suggest a light touch or a payoff.
- Foreshadowing past its "planned payoff" but still unpaid: list it.
- Suspected foreshadowing in the text that the table does not register: list it and ask whether to register it.

### 10. Character health
- Important characters whose last appearance is more than 10-15 chapters ago: list them and ask whether it is deliberate (some characters should leave).
- Do main characters' lines still keep their own voices? Run a name-covered test on a passage or two.
- If `追蹤/關係.md` exists: do forms of address, attitude and interaction between characters violate the currently valid (end chapter blank) relationship? Did a relationship change without a story reason, or slip back to a state already ended (look at end chapter and cause)?

### 11. Boundaries of disclosure
- Against `追蹤/時間線.md`: does the text give away what the reader should not yet know?
- If `追蹤/讀者認知.md` exists: have suspense and misbelief rows whose resolving chapter is still empty been revealed when the card did not ask for it? Has a deliberately planted misbelief been pierced by careless narration?
- Any "little did he know", "what she didn't realize was" or "years later" lines that cross the disclosure boundary?

### 12. Reader contract and pacing
- Against the reader contract in `設定/故事核心.md`: any breach?
- Emotion over the last few chapters: more than three chapters of suppression without release? Do several chapters in a row share the same function or hook type?
- Is the lead's credit often taken at no cost?

## Severity

| Level | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 必須修 (must fix) | An error or contradiction a reader will notice | injury vanishes, a character knows what he could not, setting contradiction, early spoiler |
| 建議修 (should fix) | Weakens the reading experience | a transition that loses the reader, an emotional turn too fast, an uncaught hook, foreshadowing left hanging |
| 提醒 (note) | Worth watching | dialogue voices converging slightly, the same hook type two chapters in a row |

If two or more of the six in-chapter items are "must fix", the chapter does not pass.
