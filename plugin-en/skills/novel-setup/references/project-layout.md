# Novel project structure and database specification

This specification is the common foundation of the whole novel-* skill set. novel-setup builds folders by it, and every other skill reads and writes files by its paths.

**Important:** folder names, file names, YAML keys, table headers and the fixed words in tables (statuses, types) stay in Chinese exactly as written here, because the novel studio program reads them. The content you write into them (names, descriptions, summaries, notes) is in English.

## 1. Folder structure

```
{Title}/
├── CLAUDE.md                  ← rules for this book (loaded at the start of every conversation)
├── bible/                     ← static settings: change rarely, consulted before writing
│   ├── story-core.md          genre, selling points, one-line hook, story engine, reader contract, ending direction
│   ├── style.md               this book's style: viewpoint, sentence rhythm, taboos, example passages
│   ├── author-prefs.md        habits the author has said "from now on" about
│   ├── glossary.md            proper nouns, forms of address, fixed spellings
│   ├── world/{topic}.md       one topic per file: geography, history, power system, social structure …
│   ├── characters/{name}.md   one person per file: static profile
│   ├── factions/{name}.md     one organization per file
│   └── drafts/                unconfirmed settings (AI-generated, extracted from conversation); moved to the official folders once confirmed
├── outline/                   ← plans: what has not yet happened
│   ├── book.md
│   ├── volumes/vol-01.md      one file per volume: volume contract and plot units
│   └── chapters/ch-0001.md    one file per chapter
├── manuscript/
│   └── ch-0001.md
├── tracking/                  ← facts that have happened: updated only after a chapter is written
│   ├── state-card.md          the current situation, required reading before every chapter
│   ├── characters/{name}.md   dynamic snapshot: location, injuries, holdings, what they know
│   ├── foreshadowing.md       foreshadowing table
│   ├── timeline.md            the author's truth and what the reader knows
│   ├── reader-knowledge.md    what the reader knows, suspects and misbelieves (optional; created during write-back)
│   ├── relations.md           who stands where toward whom, with history (optional; created during write-back)
│   └── summaries/ch-0001.md   one short summary per chapter
├── candidates/                candidates: chapters written but not yet adopted by the author (when using the review screen)
├── samples/                   passages the author likes, used as positive style examples (optional)
└── _templates/                blank templates for each kind of file (copied in by novel-setup)
```

Chapter files are named ch-0001.md (the chapter number zero-padded to four digits); the chapter title goes in the first-line heading (`# 第1章 Title`), not in the file name. Characters, factions and places are filed under their English names (bible/characters/Ines Marlow.md).

## 2. Three iron rules

1. **Keep plans and facts apart.** The outline and cards say "what is meant to happen"; tracking says "what has already happened in the text". Tracking is not changed before the text is written, and an outline plan is never cited as established fact.
2. **Keep settings and state apart.** `bible/characters/` holds the profile that rarely changes (personality, looks, background, way of speaking); `tracking/characters/` holds what may change every chapter (where they are, what injuries, what they carry, which secrets they know, how they stand with whom).
3. **Keep drafts and official apart.** Settings generated by AI or organised from conversation that the author has not approved always go to `bible/drafts/` first. They move to the official folders only after the author confirms. Chapter text may cite only official settings.

## 3. YAML frontmatter specification

Every database file starts with a YAML block, for searching and checking. Write `null` or an empty list for a missing value; do not delete the key.

### Character (bible/characters/{name}.md)

```yaml
---
type: character
name: Ines Marlow
aliases: [Nessie, Miss Ines]
role: protagonist          # protagonist / main-supporting / supporting / antagonist / minor
first_appearance: 1
status: canon          # canon / draft
---
```

### Faction, worldbuilding

```yaml
---
type: faction          # or world
name: The Quay Guild
status: canon
related_characters: [Ines Marlow, Callum Brandt]
---
```

### Chapter card (outline/chapters/ch-0001.md)

```yaml
---
type: chapter-card
chapter: 1
title: The Day the Engagement Ended
volume: 1
target_length: 2500      # may be empty
status: approved        # draft / approved / written
---
```

### Character state (tracking/characters/{name}.md)

```yaml
---
type: character-state
name: Ines Marlow
updated_through: 12
last_appearance: 12
---
```

### Chapter summary (tracking/summaries/ch-0001.md)

```yaml
---
type: chapter-summary
chapter: 1
title: The Day the Engagement Ended
length: 2600
---
```

(For an English book the `length` field counts words.)

## 4. How to write the tracking files

### tracking/state-card.md (fixed seven sections; keep the whole file under 1,500 words)

1. **Where we are**: which volume, which chapter just finished, in-story time, main setting
2. **Main-line progress**: how far the main line has got and what the next goal is
3. **Characters present**: the characters now at the centre of the story, one sentence of news each
4. **Promises for the next chapter**: what the last chapter's ending leaves for the next chapter to catch
5. **Long-term constraints**: rules to keep through the whole book (e.g. the lead cannot reveal her identity before volume three)
6. **Continuity risks**: error-prone details (e.g. Callum's left hand is still injured)
7. **Active foreshadowing**: IDs of foreshadowing to advance or pay off now, with one line each

The state card is a snapshot of "now", not a history. Delete outdated content directly; history stays in the chapter summaries.

### tracking/foreshadowing.md (one table)

| 編號 | 內容 | 埋設章 | 預計回收 | 最後出現 | 狀態 | 重要度 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F001 | The half ferry token her mother left | 第001章 | end of volume one | 第008章 | 推進中 | 高 |

Status must be one of: 已埋, 推進中, 已回收, 廢棄. Importance: 高, 中, 低. "Planned payoff" holds only the one chapter you mean to pay it off in (第NNN章); leave it empty if undecided. Arrangements like "advance from chapter N" or "echo in chapter N" go in the content column or the outline, otherwise the studio would treat them as the payoff chapter when judging overdue items (the studio skips such wording, but a bare chapter number is the most reliable).

### tracking/timeline.md (the author's truth and what the reader knows, in separate columns)

| 編號 | 故事時間 | 實際發生（作者真相） | 讀者目前知道 | 揭示章 | 相關角色 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E001 | Three winters ago | Ines's father was framed by the Brandts | Only knows her father went to prison | 未揭示 | her father, old Mr. Brandt |

"What the reader knows now" is the information boundary while writing: the text must not let a character or the narrator say what the reader should not yet know.

### tracking/reader-knowledge.md (the reader's state of mind, optional)

| 編號 | 類型 | 內容 | 作者真相 | 種下章 | 回收章 | 相關角色 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R001 | 懸念 | Why did her father plead guilty? | The Brandts threatened her mother's life | 第003章 |  | her father, old Mr. Brandt |
| R002 | 誤認 | The reader thinks young Brandt sent the letter | It was the Brandts' old servant | 第005章 | 第012章 | Callum |

Type must be one of: 已知 (the reader knows, but some characters in the story do not yet), 懸念 (a question the reader waits to have answered), 誤認 (what the reader wrongly believes). A blank resolving chapter means not yet revealed; the author's truth is optional. The timeline records events; this table records the reader's questions and misunderstandings.

### tracking/relations.md (between characters, who toward whom, optional)

| 編號 | 誰 | 對誰 | 關係 | 態度 | 起始章 | 結束章 | 變化原因 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L001 | Callum | Ines | classmates | dismissive | 第003章 | 第020章 | first meeting |
| L002 | Callum | Ines | allies | respectful | 第020章 |  | Ines took the blame for him |
| L003 | Ines | Callum | classmates | wary | 第003章 |  | first meeting |

One direction per row: A toward B and B toward A are separate, and the two views may differ. When a relationship changes, put the changing chapter in the old row's end chapter and open a new row; never overwrite, so the history stays. A blank end chapter means currently valid. When writing, look only at the currently valid rows among characters on stage; when reviewing, read the history too, to catch "changed without a cause" or "slipped back".

### tracking/summaries/ch-NNNN.md (100-250 words)

Four things: what happened in the chapter, how the situation changed, what and who newly appeared, where the chapter stops.

## 5. Who owns each file

| File | Main writer | When |
|---|---|---|
| bible/story-core.md, style.md | novel-setup, novel-outline | at setup, when changing direction |
| bible/author-prefs.md | any skill | when the author clearly says "from now on …" |
| bible/characters, factions, world, glossary.md, drafts | novel-bible | when building settings, and registering new things after a chapter |
| outline/* | novel-outline | when planning |
| manuscript/* | novel-write | when writing chapters |
| tracking/* | novel-write (write-back after writing), novel-review (fixing errors) | after the chapter text is done |

Tracking files are updated only once the chapter text is final. When the author revises a chapter already written, use novel-write's revision flow and fix tracking along with it.
