# 作者偏好

> Record only habits the author has said out loud and wants kept long-term (e.g. "from now on …", "I usually …").
> Do not record patterns the AI guessed from edits, nor one-off requests like "for this chapter …".
> One preference per line; put exceptions and limits on the same line, do not split them.
