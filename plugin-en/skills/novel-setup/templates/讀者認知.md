# 讀者認知

> What the reader believes so far: 已知 is what the reader knows; 懸念 is a question the reader is waiting to have answered; 誤認 is what the reader wrongly believes (for reversals).
> "Author's truth" is the answer (optional); a blank resolving chapter means not yet revealed. When writing, do not resolve a suspense or misbelief before its time.
> Keep the type words 已知 / 懸念 / 誤認 in Chinese exactly as given; the studio reads them.
